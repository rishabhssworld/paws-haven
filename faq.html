Adoption FAQ

How old do I have to be to adopt?

Adopters must be 18 or older.

Will someone visit my home?

Yes. A friendly volunteer visits before the adoption is finalized, so we can make sure the match is a good one.

What does adopting cost?

Fees cover vaccinations, spaying or neutering, and a microchip. We will go over the exact amount with you in person.
